Fix the map experience and venue interaction.

Current issues:
- Venue pins are not accurate enough
- Heat map is too large and too blended
- It is hard to tell which exact venue is busy
- Clicking one pin shows multiple venues, which is confusing
- Bottom sheet covers too much of the screen
- Trackpad map controls feel wrong on MacBook

Make these changes:

1. Accurate venue locations
- Every venue must have a real latitude and longitude
- Pins must sit exactly on the venue location, not just the suburb
- Add/edit venue data so each venue includes:
  - name
  - full address
  - latitude
  - longitude
- Use the exact coordinates for each venue where possible

2. Pin behaviour
- When I tap/click a venue pin, show ONLY that exact venue
- Do not show nearby venues when a single pin is selected
- If I tap a heat area with no exact pin, then show nearby venues
- Make the selected pin visually obvious

3. Heat map behaviour
- Reduce heat radius
- Make heat tighter and more precise around each venue
- Heat should still softly spill into the nearby street area
- Heat should not cover the whole suburb
- Make it easy to tell which venue is hottest
- Use separate heat pockets when venues are far enough apart
- Only blend heat when venues are very close together

4. Street energy
- Add a subtle street-level glow around nightlife streets
- This should represent people outside venues, lines, foot traffic, and street energy
- Keep it lighter than the venue heat
- Venue heat should still be the strongest signal

5. Visual heat style
- Keep the dark grey GTA-style map
- Use smaller thermal heat blobs
- Strongest heat should have a hot core
- Outer heat should fade smoothly
- Avoid huge blurry circles

6. Map controls
- Improve MacBook trackpad behaviour
- Two-finger swipe should pan the map, not zoom
- Pinch should zoom
- Click and drag should also still pan
- Disable scroll-wheel zoom if needed
- Make map movement feel like Apple Maps or Google Maps

7. Replace bottom sheet
- Do not use a large bottom sheet on desktop/tablet
- On wider screens, show selected venue info in a compact side panel on the right
- On mobile, use a smaller bottom card that does not cover too much of the map
- The venue panel should show only:
  - venue image
  - venue name
  - address
  - heat level as 🔥 icons
  - entry price
  - line/wait time
  - closing status
  - buttons: Going, Here Now, Left

8. Keep everything fast
- Reduce reading
- Use icons and short labels
- Make the map the main focus
- Make the selected venue clear immediately
